What is an Electric Vehicle?

An Electric Vehicle, or EV, is a vehicle that runs using an electric motor instead of mainly using a petrol or diesel engine.

EVs use rechargeable batteries to store electricity. This electricity powers the motor and moves the vehicle.

EVs are becoming an important part of cleaner transportation.

🚗

Battery + Motor = EV

Types of Electric Vehicles

🔋

BEV

Battery Electric Vehicle

Runs completely using electricity stored in a battery.

⚡

PHEV

Plug-in Hybrid

Uses an electric motor and a petrol engine.

🔄

HEV

Hybrid Electric Vehicle

Combines an electric motor with a normal engine.

Benefits of Electric Vehicles

🌱

Less Tailpipe Pollution

Battery-electric vehicles do not produce tailpipe emissions while driving.

💰

Running Cost

Electricity may cost less than petrol for some journeys.

🔇

Less Noise

Electric motors are generally quieter than engines.

⚡

Energy Efficient

Electric motors efficiently convert electricity into movement.

Challenges of EVs

🔌 Charging

More charging stations are needed.

⏱️ Charging Time

Charging can take longer than refuelling.

🔋 Battery Cost

Batteries are an important part of an EV and can be costly.

♻️ Recycling

Used batteries need proper recycling and management.
